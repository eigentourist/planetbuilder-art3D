# Notes for logo 005-001

## Interpretation

- Begin design version 005 while preserving the final shape and separate-character construction established in 004-015.
- Replace the former single 1400 W area light with three soft area lights whose 1.0, 0.3, and 0.5 intensities are treated as ratios against that established 1400 W reference.
- Place a large key above and left of the camera and slightly closer to the logo, a large fill slightly above and closer than the camera, and a medium rim behind and above the logo.
- Use starting disk sizes of 8.0, 9.0, and 4.5 Blender units respectively, leaving exact placement and size available for later visual iteration.

## Observations

The three-light arrangement reveals the shallow extrusion and bevels more clearly than the previous lighting. A warm highlight is visible along the upper edges and on several left-facing surfaces, particularly across the left and middle characters. The fill preserves readable orange front faces without harsh cast shadows or strong specular hotspots. The rear rim contribution is comparatively subtle from this camera angle; adjusting its position in a later experiment may produce clearer separation along the upper-right silhouette.

The inherited shape remains legible and unclipped, with 13 separate character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate, so no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 004-015

The geometry, framing, and character placement match 004-015. In 005-001 the lettering appears brighter and more dimensional: upper bevels carry distinct warm highlights, front-face illumination varies more gradually across the word, and the extrusion is easier to perceive. The guides retain a soft cream appearance, although their relatively smooth response makes the new rim light less obvious than it is on the lettering.
