# Notes — 004-010

## Interpretation

Retain the successful construction from experiment 004-009—positive 30-degree X pitch, positive 15-degree Y yaw shared with the guides, continuous mesh fitting, and a final shared Z rotation—but reduce the final Z roll from 20 to 10 degrees. Return the perspective camera from 26 Blender units to its earlier 21-unit distance to determine whether the milder rolled composition remains fully framed.

## Observations

The 10-degree roll produces a more balanced result. The left side remains visibly lower and the right side higher, reinforcing the rising trajectory, but the in-plane rotation no longer dominates the logo's established curvature. Native Days One spacing and the fitted relationship between lettering and guides remain intact.

The camera can successfully return to its earlier 21-unit position. The complete assembly remains within the 1200×675 frame without clipping, including the lower-left guide endpoint and upper-right guide endpoint. The closer camera lets the logo occupy substantially more of the canvas than in experiment 004-009 and makes the letterforms easier to evaluate.

The lettering remains at 8,320 vertices and approximately 13,284 triangles, below the provisional 25,000-triangle target. No GLB or texture files were produced.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-004-009.png`, experiment 004-010 halves the final roll and restores the earlier camera distance. The 20-degree version had a strong diagonal attitude and required wider framing; the 10-degree version is larger on screen, retains comfortable edge margins, and integrates the rise more naturally with the logarithmic guide shape. Experiment 004-010 is the stronger framing and orientation balance.
