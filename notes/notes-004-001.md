# Notes — 004-001

## Interpretation

Begin design version 004 with the first lettering test. Set `PLANETBUILDER` in uppercase and fit all thirteen glyphs within the negative space defined by the version 003 guides. Choose an available font that supports the retro science-fiction direction, place letters individually along the trajectory, rotate them with the local flow, and scale their height according to the changing distance between the upper and lower guides.

Goldman Bold was selected for this first test. Its compact width accommodates the long title, while its squared curves, cut corners, and technological construction suggest mid-century-to-late-century retro futurism without becoming difficult to read. The font choice was explicitly authorized for this initial test.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the primary goal: the complete word is legible and fits within the guide envelope. Letter heights taper from the large `P` at launch to the smaller terminal letters, and each glyph rotates with the average local direction of the two guides. This makes the word feel carried by the established launch-to-orbit trajectory rather than merely placed over it.

Goldman Bold is a promising initial choice. Its substantial strokes survive the decreasing height on the right, and its angular details suit the game’s retro science-fiction identity. The solid warm orange is intentionally simple for this shape-and-fit test and separates the lettering clearly from the cream guides.

The largest weakness is spacing. Glyph centers are distributed at equal intervals, so narrow characters—most visibly the `I`—leave excessive gaps, while wide characters occupy their slots more fully. The rhythm around `BUILDER` is consequently uneven. A subsequent experiment should calculate cumulative placement from each scaled glyph’s actual width and introduce controlled tracking, while continuing to derive height and rotation from the guide envelope. The strong reduction in letter height toward the right is faithful to the available space but may also merit moderation after spacing is improved.

No GLB or texture files were produced. The letters remain editable Blender text objects because the font and final geometry have not yet been approved.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-003-001.png`, this preview preserves the same 3D ribbon guides, perspective framing, dark physically lit cream, and black environment while adding the first actual logo content. Version 003 established only the envelope; version 004 demonstrates that the full title can occupy it successfully. The orange letters now dominate the composition as intended, and the guide darkness is less problematic because the guides function as secondary construction references rather than finished artwork.
