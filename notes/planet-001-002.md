## Observations

The planet now occupies approximately one third of the 1200×675 frame height, bringing the composition into closer agreement with the current design guidance. It remains fully visible with ample black space around the silhouette and no cropping.

The closer view makes the dark-green surface patterns, bright organic-looking clusters, and combined normal/bump relief easier to evaluate. The 12,096-triangle silhouette remains smooth, and no longitudinal seam is apparent. Lighting and material response remain subdued but readable, with no new hot spots or clipping introduced by the camera change.

The increased apparent size strengthens the planet's visual presence and better supports its role as the larger of the two celestial assets. The existing lighting still leaves the left and lower edges heavily shadowed, which can be addressed independently in a later experiment if desired.
