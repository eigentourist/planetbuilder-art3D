# Notes for logo 004-015

## Interpretation

- Create one Days One text object for each of the 13 characters in `PLANETBUILDER`.
- Preserve the font's established positioning by placing each character from the measured right edge of its progressively longer full-string prefix, then center the complete line.
- Convert every character into its own mesh.
- Apply positive 20-degree X and positive 15-degree Y rotations to all character meshes about one shared lettering pivot, and rotate the guides positive 15 degrees around Y.
- Fit all character meshes collectively into the guide corridor using the established 15% vertical margins.
- Widen each character about its own center, tapering from 1.18 at the left to 1.0 at the right, so the widening does not increase inter-character spacing.
- Rotate the guides and all character meshes together positive 4 degrees around Z.

## Observations

The result contains exactly 13 independently selectable character meshes and remains clearly legible. It closely preserves the established Days One spacing and overall silhouette while providing reliable character-level control. The left-side characters fill more horizontal space, the right side remains comparatively unchanged, and no obvious character collisions or guide clipping are visible. The character meshes total approximately 13,284 triangles, unchanged from the combined-mesh implementation and below the provisional 25,000-triangle logo limit.

Days One continues to suit the design; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 004-013

The visual composition is similar, but 004-015 replaces inferred topology bands with an explicit one-object-per-character construction. This removes the nine-band grouping problem seen in 004-013 and ensures that each width adjustment is centered on a complete character. Compared with the preferred appearance of 004-012, the result remains visually consistent while providing a much cleaner and more dependable structure for subsequent spacing work.
