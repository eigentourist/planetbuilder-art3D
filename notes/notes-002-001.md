# Notes — 002-001

## Interpretation

Begin design version 002 by carrying forward the approved `001-004` trajectory and splitting it into two identical cream lines, one directly above the other, on the same black background. Treat “spacing” as the clear vertical gap between the stroke edges. Set that gap to twice the diameter of one stroke, while keeping the pair centered around the preceding single-line position.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the primary goal of this new design iteration. Both lines retain identical Bézier geometry, thickness, color, and horizontal timing. Their parallel relationship is immediately legible, and the black interval is wide enough to keep the strokes distinct without separating them into unrelated elements.

The vertical edge-to-edge gap is exactly twice one line's thickness. Because the lines are identical vertical translations rather than perpendicular offsets, the shortest visible gap is somewhat narrower in the steep launch region on the left. This does not undermine the requested “one above the other” construction and gives the pair a slightly tighter, energetic launch before it opens into a clearly measured horizontal spacing. No font recommendation is relevant at this line-only stage.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-001-004.png`, the trajectory geometry, warm cream color, medium stroke weight, framing, and black background are unchanged. The new preview replaces the single baseline with a vertically centered pair. This begins version 002 with a clear two-line framework while preserving the successful launch-to-level-flight silhouette established by the final preview of version 001.
