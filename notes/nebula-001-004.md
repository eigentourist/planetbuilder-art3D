# Nebula 001-004

## Interpretation

Use the three grayscale noise images from the existing game as broad organic masks while retaining restrained procedural noise for finer internal variation. Bake the interaction into three square transparent cloud layers so Godot only needs to rotate, scale, and modulate inexpensive sprites. Preserve the current dark exposure and central negative space while breaking up the lower-right horizontal amber band.

## Observations

The historical masks materially improve the cloud silhouettes. The lower-right amber region is no longer a long regular stripe; it breaks into broad, irregular concentrations with soft transitions. Violet and red structure across the upper-left also looks more cloud-like, and the large central dark region remains suitable for the logo and foreground assets.

The composite is darker and more subdued than experiment 001-003. This gives it a useful background role and avoids competing with the title assets, but a later experiment may benefit from a small visibility increase if the complete title-screen composition makes the nebula too recessive.

The 4:3 historical masks were center-cropped into the square output layers without distortion. The resulting square RGBA layers remain suitable for rotation with viewport-diagonal runtime scaling in Godot.

## Comparison with 001-003

Experiment 001-003 established better coverage and softer boundaries but retained obvious horizontal banding, especially in the amber lower-right region. Experiment 001-004 trades some brightness for substantially more organic, asymmetric structure. It better satisfies the current shape-refinement goal and provides a stronger basis for later visibility tuning.
