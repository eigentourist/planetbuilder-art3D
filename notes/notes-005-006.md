# Notes for logo 005-006

## Interpretation

- Preserve the character construction, shared rotations, guide-based fitting, three-light rig, closer camera, and camera-relative key and fill positions from 005-005.
- Continue using both guide centerlines and both ribbon guide meshes during construction and fitting.
- After the 13 character meshes are fitted, remove all four curved guide objects from the scene.
- Apply the final positive 4-degree Z rotation to the character meshes alone.

## Observations

The saved scene and preview contain only the fitted lettering, lights, camera, and shared lettering pivot; all four curved guide objects were removed. The character meshes retain the established rising trajectory without needing the guides to communicate the arc. Removing the cream lines gives the orange lettering greater visual prominence and makes its standalone silhouette and lighting response easier to evaluate.

The logo remains unclipped at the closer camera distance. Soft highlights continue to reveal the bevels without harsh shadows or strong hotspots. The scene retains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-005

Character geometry, placement, lighting, and framing are unchanged. Experiment 005-005 displayed the curved guides as visible cream ribbons surrounding the word, while 005-006 uses them only as construction aids and removes them before output. Without the guides, the lettering feels less enclosed and more clearly functions as a standalone logo, although the empty black space below the rising left side becomes more visually apparent.
