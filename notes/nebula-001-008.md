## Interpretation

Investigate the marked parallelogram-like dark contour in the upper-left purple cloud. Preserve all experiment 001-007 masks, contrast, blur, transforms, exposure, and cloud02/cloud03 color sampling. ## Observations

The narrow adjustment preserves the successful overall composition. It changes the local purple color distribution in the marked area without moving cloud01's silhouette or altering the darker negative-space treatment established in experiment 001-007.

The parallelogram-like contour remains visible at full production resolution, although its internal tonal pattern is slightly less coherent. This means cloud01 contributes to the appearance but is not its sole cause. The contour is likely reinforced by an overlapping color or alpha feature from cloud02 or cloud03 rather than being a single feature carried entirely by cloud01.

## Comparison with 001-007

Experiment 001-008 differs only subtly from experiment 001-007, as intended. The upper-left purple field changes while the rest of the composite remains stable. The adjustment is safe but insufficient as a complete correction. A subsequent focused experiment should hold this cloud01 rotation and independently perturb the color sampling of the next most likely overlapping layer, rather than increasing global blur or changing the masks.
