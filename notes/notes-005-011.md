# Notes for logo 005-011

## Interpretation

- Preserve the relaxed left-hand curvature, right-hand convergence, separate character meshes, shared X/Y orientation, lighting, closer camera, and guide-removal workflow from 005-010.
- Remove the final shared Z rotation by setting it to zero degrees.
- Interpret the permitted 3% guide violation as 3% of the local vertical distance between the upper and lower guides.
- Map the outer fitted font bounds from 3% below the lower guide to 3% above the upper guide, retaining the internal proportional relationships of the Days One meshes.

## Observations

The lettering becomes substantially taller and more assertive because it now occupies 106% of the local guide corridor rather than the previous 70%. Font details remain legible, and no visible frame clipping or character collision occurs. Without the final Z roll, the composition's upward trajectory comes entirely from the fitted guide shape, producing a more direct view of the underlying relaxed curve.

The larger letter faces make the lighting easier to read, with broad warm illumination and soft edge transitions. The scene retains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-010

Experiment 005-010 retained 15% clearance at both guide boundaries and applied a final positive 4-degree Z rotation. Experiment 005-011 instead permits 3% overshoot at each boundary and uses no final Z rotation. As a result, 005-011 has much taller character forms and a less globally tilted presentation. The relaxed left curvature, right-side convergence, lighting, and camera distance remain unchanged.
