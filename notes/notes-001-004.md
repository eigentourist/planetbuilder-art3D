# Notes — 001-004

## Interpretation

Retain the established cream trajectory line on black, but begin its deliberate leveling transition as it enters the final 40 percent of its length. Complete that transition at the start of the final 10 percent, leaving the last tenth horizontal rather than waiting until the endpoint to reach a horizontal tangent.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the revised timing instruction. The initial 60 percent preserves the rising launch character, the following 30 percent eases gently out of the ascent, and the final 10 percent holds a horizontal course. The transition is continuous and does not introduce a visible corner at either boundary. This creates a clearer sense that the trajectory has already achieved orbital flight before it ends.

The complete line remains well framed, and its warm ivory color and medium weight continue to read clearly against black. The curve retains its asymmetric, non-circular character. No font recommendation is relevant during this baseline-only design phase.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-001-003.png`, the leveling process begins earlier and the curve becomes horizontal sooner. Experiment 001-003 continued a slight ascent almost to its endpoint and achieved a horizontal tangent only as the line ended. Experiment 001-004 instead provides a short but legible horizontal final run, making the transition from ascent to level flight more explicit while preserving the overall silhouette.
