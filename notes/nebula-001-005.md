# Nebula 001-005

## Interpretation

Repeat the experiment 001-004 hybrid-mask recipe without changing its cloud placement, mask parameters, exposure, or compositing. Replace `textures/example-base-layer-4k.png` with `textures/example-base-layer-blurred-4k.png` as the shared color field so the effect of source-image blur can be evaluated independently.

## Observations

The blurred source produces smoother broad color transitions, most visibly in the violet upper-left cloud and the amber lower portion of the composition. Fine cloud texture remains because the historical masks and procedural detail still contribute variation. The blur therefore reduces the grain originating in the color field without making the clouds uniformly soft or featureless.

The new source is 3940×2260 rather than 3840×2160 and contains rounded dark corners. The cloud-layer center crop samples the interior, so neither issue is visibly harmful in this composite. The generated production background and composite retain the required 3840×2160 output dimensions. A future cleanup of the source to exact 16:9 dimensions with square corners would make the reference background more predictable, but is not required for the current masked-cloud result.

## Comparison with 001-004

The composition, organic silhouettes, central negative space, and overall exposure are effectively unchanged from experiment 001-004, as intended. Experiment 001-005 looks marginally cleaner and less grainy in its large color regions while retaining small-scale cloud character. The blurred source is the preferable basis for subsequent iterations.
