# Notes for logo 004-012

## Interpretation

- Retain the 20-degree X rotation and 15-degree Y rotation from 004-011.
- Restore the established 15% top and bottom corridor margins so this experiment emphasizes horizontal rather than vertical occupancy.
- Widen the lettering progressively from a factor of 1.18 at the left to 1.0 at the right, without shifting character centers or deliberately increasing inter-letter spacing.
- Reduce the final shared positive Z rotation to 4 degrees.

## Observations

The preview is legible and the broader left-side forms use more of the horizontal corridor. The mesh remains approximately 13,284 triangles. However, inspection of the generation output revealed 45 disconnected mesh islands rather than the expected 13 character groups. Scaling those islands independently can treat separate surfaces within one glyph inconsistently, even though no obvious breakage is visible in this preview. This run is therefore retained as an informative intermediate experiment rather than the preferred implementation.

Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 004-011

The left-hand letters appear broader, while the restored vertical margins make the text less vertically dominant than 004-011. The 4-degree roll is subtly flatter than the prior 5-degree roll. A follow-up should group overlapping mesh islands into complete glyphs before applying the same widening gradient.
