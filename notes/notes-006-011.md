## Observations

The 40-degree yaw makes the three-dimensional orientation much more pronounced. Visible front faces become narrower, and side depth is easier to read, particularly through the red and orange characters. The left-near/right-far progression feels stronger, while the cream end remains sufficiently broad and legible under the soft lighting.

Because arc placement still happens afterward, the two-dimensional radius-ten baseline silhouette and character spacing remain stable. The logo stays centered and unclipped, with approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains suitable; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 006-010

Only the shared Y rotation changes, increasing from positive 20 to positive 40 degrees. Experiment 006-010 balances readable front faces with moderate depth. Experiment 006-011 emphasizes extrusion, side planes, and spatial recession more strongly, at the cost of narrower frontal silhouettes. The material gradient, lighting response, arc span, and framing remain otherwise unchanged.
