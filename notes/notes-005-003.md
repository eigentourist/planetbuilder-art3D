# Notes for logo 005-003

## Interpretation

- Preserve the 005-001 construction, shape, guide orientation, lighting, and framing.
- Rotate all character centers and geometry positive 15 degrees around Y using the shared lettering pivot.
- Add a further positive 2-degree Y rotation around the individual centers of only the P, L, and A meshes.
- Leave the other ten characters without an individual yaw adjustment.

## Observations

P, L, and A have an effective positive 17-degree facing yaw while the remaining characters retain the shared positive 15-degree yaw. The selected adjustment is subtle but adds slightly more visible side depth and differentiated edge lighting to those three left-side letters. The logo remains legible, no obvious new collisions or guide clipping are visible, and the mesh total remains approximately 13,284 triangles.

The selected letters again participate in the front-to-back visual progression rather than facing parallel to the camera, but their additional two degrees do not create a severe overlap effect at this scale. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-002

Experiment 005-002 applied negative 15-degree yaw compensation to every character, leaving their faces mutually parallel despite the shared positional yaw. Experiment 005-003 removes that compensation: all characters again carry the shared positive 15-degree facing yaw, with P, L, and A rotated an additional positive 2 degrees. Consequently, 005-003 shows more side depth and left-to-right foreground ordering, while 005-002 reads flatter and more uniformly front-facing. Lighting and framing are unchanged, so the orientation difference is directly comparable.
