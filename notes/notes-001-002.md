# Notes — 001-002

## Interpretation

Establish the logo's baseline before introducing letterforms. The baseline is interpreted as a left-to-right launch trajectory: it rises most strongly on the left, then continuously relaxes toward near-horizontal travel on the right. Render it at medium thickness in a warm, slightly orange cream against a pure black background, evoking a 1950s drugstore cream soda.

## Observations

The preview captures the intended asymmetric, logarithmic character. Its left side has clear upward momentum, while the long rightward easing suggests transition into orbital flight rather than a symmetrical circular arc. The simple black field makes the silhouette easy to judge, and the warm ivory color reads as aged cream rather than stark white. The line is smooth and substantial enough to serve as a visible letter baseline without feeling like a heavy graphic bar. Both ends continue beyond the frame, keeping attention on the trajectory rather than on decorative endpoints.

This preview fulfills the aim of the current design iteration and provides a useful baseline shape for later placement of letterforms. A future adjustment may be warranted once actual letters reveal whether the steep left portion produces excessive rotation or vertical displacement, but no font change is relevant at this shape-only stage.

## Comparison

Experiment 001-001 did not produce a preview because Blender stopped on an unsupported render-engine identifier. There is therefore no earlier image to compare visually. Experiment 001-002 is the first successfully rendered baseline and establishes the comparison point for subsequent experiments.
