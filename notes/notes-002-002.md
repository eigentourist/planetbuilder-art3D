# Notes — 002-002

## Interpretation

Develop the paired-line framework by moving the lower baseline downward by two stroke widths and broadening the upper line's arc by approximately 20 percent. Interpret a wider arc as a larger effective curvature radius: reduce the upper line's vertical excursion by a factor of `1/1.2` while anchoring its final height. This should produce a generous opening on the left that gradually converges toward two horizontal, parallel lines on the right.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the stated design goal. The two lines begin with substantially more open space between them, creating a strong wedge-like launch area, and the negative space narrows continuously as the trajectories move right. Both lines finish horizontally, so the convergence resolves into a clearly parallel relationship rather than closing to a point.

The upper contour is visibly broader and less steep than the lower baseline while remaining smooth and compatible with the established launch-to-orbit silhouette. The lower line's two-stroke-width displacement creates useful interior room for eventual letterforms. The warm cream color, medium stroke weight, black background, and full-line framing remain unchanged. No font recommendation is necessary at this contour-development stage.
