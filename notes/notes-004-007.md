# Notes — 004-007

## Interpretation

Create the complete Days One title as one 3D text object, convert it to a mesh, apply a positive 35-degree X rotation to reduce the preceding pitch, then apply a positive 15-degree Y rotation so the left side advances toward the camera and the right side recedes. Apply the same positive 15-degree Y orientation to both visible ribbon guides and hidden centerline guides, then fit the rotated text mesh into the projected negative space between the yawed guide paths.

## Observations

The combined rotations fulfill the intended effect. Reducing pitch from 45 to 35 degrees retains visible face depth and bevel shading while making the tilt less conspicuous. The positive Y yaw adds modest horizontal foreshortening: the left side feels nearer and more prominent, while the right side recedes without calling attention to the rotation itself.

Yawing the guides with the lettering keeps the construction envelope visually coherent. The complete title remains readable, the native Days One spacing remains intact, and the established launch-to-orbit silhouette survives the added perspective. The guide endpoints contract horizontally as expected under yaw, but continue to frame the word appropriately.

The fitted lettering remains at 8,320 vertices and approximately 13,284 triangles, comfortably below the provisional 25,000-triangle target. No GLB or texture files were produced.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-004-006.png`, the new preview has slightly less pitch-driven top-surface emphasis and a clearer sense of depth running from left to right. Experiment 004-006 treated both sides symmetrically in depth; experiment 004-007 advances the large launch-side letters and lets the smaller terminal letters recede. The difference is restrained, which supports the instruction to enhance three-dimensional presence without sacrificing the established silhouette.
