# Notes for logo 006-002

## Interpretation

- Preserve the continuous logo-wide material coordinates, geometry, camera, and three-light rig from 006-001.
- Replace the deep-red starting stop with a brighter saturated red while retaining orange, yellow, and cream at the remaining stops.
- Lower the four roughness values from 0.45/0.35/0.25/0.15 to 0.4/0.3/0.2/0.1.
- Keep metallic and emission disabled and retain the restrained specular setting.

## Observations

The left side now reads more clearly as red rather than near-maroon, improving its chromatic connection to the orange transition through PLANET. Lower roughness produces somewhat clearer upper-edge and bevel reflections across the word. The cream region is the smoothest and shows the strongest response, but the large light sources keep the highlights broad rather than point-like.

The gradient remains continuous across all 13 meshes, and the logo remains legible and unclipped. Geometry remains approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 006-001

Geometry, gradient positions, lighting, and framing are unchanged. Experiment 006-002 brightens the first color stop and lowers every roughness stop by 0.05. Compared with 006-001, the red is more recognizable and the bevel response is slightly more pronounced, especially in the yellow and cream regions. The transition remains balanced across the complete word without visible seams between character objects.
