# Nebula 001-002 Notes

## Interpretation

Use `textures/example-base-layer-4k.png` directly as the shared colorful field for all three cloud layers. Let Godot supply black behind the layers. Replace the isotropic, broadly translucent masks from nebula 001-001 with higher-contrast and directionally distinct masks: one broad horizontal structure, one diagonal structure, and one narrower warped filament structure. Bake each result as a 2048×2048 RGBA image, composite the first two normally over black, and give the filament layer a restrained brightness increase. Retain a 4K static composite and a 1200×675 preview.

## Observations

The new layers preserve the broad spectrum color movement of the supplied reference while creating genuine transparent gaps. Their directional structures are clearly differentiated, eliminating the symmetrical, isotropic feeling of the preceding experiment. The composite now has extensive true black space and a very quiet center suitable for the logo. It also avoids the magenta wash that dominated nebula 001-001.

The correction is too strong artistically. Most of the frame is nearly black, the visible structures are dim, and the high-contrast masks produce hard-edged horizontal or diagonal islands rather than diffuse nebular clouds. The top bands are especially linear. Cloud 03 suggests filaments more clearly than before, but its forms still read more like stretched mask fragments than broad ephemeral gas.

This result confirms that masking the 4K color field is the correct architecture. The next experiment should move the density ramp thresholds slightly lower, introduce wider interpolation between transparent and visible regions, raise the color-field value multipliers modestly, and reduce the most extreme anisotropic scaling. Those changes should retain black separation and directional variety while restoring diffuse cloud bodies and a usable dark exposure.

The runtime approach remains inexpensive: Godot can layer these baked RGBA images over its black background and animate only transforms, color modulation, and opacity. No procedural runtime shader is required.

## Comparison with nebula 001-001

Nebula 001-001 was bright, nearly full-frame, smooth, and magenta-dominant. Nebula 001-002 is substantially darker, more colorful, asymmetrical, and separated by black negative space. It solves uniformity and central visual competition, but overshoots into sparse, hard-edged bands. The two experiments establish useful opposite bounds for density and contrast.
