# Notes for logo 005-010

## Observations

The left end now rises more gently into the middle of the word. The trajectory remains clearly curved, but it feels less like a literal rocket path and more like a restrained suggestion of ascent. The transition through PLANET is smoother, while BUILDER retains the established near-horizontal silhouette and convergence.

The change does not introduce visible collisions or clipping at the closer camera distance. Lighting remains soft and consistent with 005-009. The scene contains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-009

Lighting, framing, mesh construction, and the right half of the guide geometry are unchanged. Compared with 005-009, the bottom of P and the early PLANET trajectory sit modestly higher, reducing the steepness and visual drop at the far left. The difference tapers away through the middle, leaving the right-hand letter placement effectively unchanged. The result preserves the overall silhouette while making its launch-trajectory reference less explicit.
