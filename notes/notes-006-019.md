# Notes for logo 006-019

## Interpretation

Retain the 20-unit circular baseline, 45-degree shared Y rotation, established spacing, warm color and roughness gradients, camera, key light, and fill light from experiment 006-018. Reduce the shared positive X rotation from 13 to 10 degrees. Move the large rim light horizontally from behind and left of the logo to directly behind its center, preserving its established height, rearward depth, intensity, size, and aim toward the logo.

## Observations

The reduced X rotation makes the logo's presentation slightly less steep without removing the upward sweep created by the circular baseline. Centering the rim light distributes its contribution more evenly across the word, producing a more continuous upper-edge highlight through the middle and right-hand characters. The highlights remain soft and do not create obvious hot spots.

The warm color progression and increasing left-to-right roughness remain clear. The logo still contains approximately 13,284 triangles, comfortably below the provisional 25,000-triangle limit. Days One remains well suited to the current retro-futurist direction; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with logo 006-018

Compared with 006-018, the baseline radius, horizontal footprint, spacing, Y-axis foreshortening, materials, camera, key light, and fill light are unchanged. The three-degree reduction in X rotation is subtle but makes the logo feel marginally more level. Moving the rim light from the rear-left to the rear-center shifts emphasis away from the far-left edge and gives the central and cream-colored characters more even upper-bevel definition.
