# Notes — 004-009

## Interpretation

Create and convert the Days One word mesh, apply positive 30-degree X pitch and positive 15-degree Y yaw, yaw the guides by the same 15 degrees, and fit the mesh into the yawed guide envelope. Only after fitting, rotate the complete assembly—including text mesh, visible ribbon guides, and hidden centerlines—positive 20 degrees around Z using one shared composition pivot. Increase camera distance enough to preserve the full rolled silhouette in frame.

## Observations

The reordered operation succeeds. Because Z rotation occurs after envelope fitting, the native spacing, glyph proportions, and legibility remain intact. The guides and lettering rotate as a single fitted assembly, so their relationship is preserved exactly.

The positive 20-degree Z rotation clearly lowers the left side and raises the right side. It reinforces the launch trajectory and gives the composition a more dynamic rising attitude. At 20 degrees, the roll is visually prominent rather than subtle, but the result remains coherent and readable. The increased camera distance keeps the entire assembly visible, though it makes the logo occupy less of the frame than in the preceding previews.

The lettering remains at 8,320 vertices and approximately 13,284 triangles, below the provisional 25,000-triangle target. No GLB or texture files were produced.
