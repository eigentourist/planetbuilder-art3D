# Nebula 001-003

## Interpretation

Soften and broaden all three procedural cloud masks while modestly increasing their visibility. Preserve the 4K example image as the shared color field, the three-layer Godot-friendly output, the opaque composite fallback, and enough black negative space for the title-screen assets.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the requested adjustment. The softened `EASE` ramps make the cloud boundaries more gradual, the less extreme anisotropic mapping produces wider forms, and the increased color-field multipliers make the red, amber, and violet regions substantially easier to read. The result still leaves a broad dark area across the upper-right and central-right portions of the frame, so it does not return to the nearly uniform opacity of experiment 001-001.

The lower-right amber form and portions of the upper-left field still read as broad horizontal bands. A later experiment could break up those silhouettes through mask rotation, distortion, or secondary low-frequency modulation while keeping the present visibility level.

## Comparison with 001-002

Experiment 001-002 was extremely dark and sparse, with narrow hard-edged bands concentrated near the top and edges. Experiment 001-003 has much greater visible coverage, smoother transitions, and a more useful distribution of source colors. It is closer to the intended diffuse nebula background, although its remaining horizontal structure should be made less regular in a future adjustment.
