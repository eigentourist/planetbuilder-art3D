# Notes for logo 004-011

## Interpretation

- Keep the Days One uppercase `PLANETBUILDER` lettering and the established guide geometry.
- Convert the text to a mesh, apply a positive 20-degree X rotation and positive 15-degree Y rotation, and apply the same Y rotation to the guides before fitting.
- Increase the lettering's corridor occupancy without changing its character proportions. The equal top and bottom fitting margins were reduced from 15% to 8% of the local guide gap, increasing nominal vertical occupancy from 70% to 84%.
- After fitting, rotate the guides and lettering together positive 5 degrees around Z using the existing shared composition pivot.
- Retain the restored camera distance of 21 used by experiment 004-010.

## Observations

The lettering remains clearly legible and follows the guide corridor without visible clipping. The smaller X rotation retains some depth while presenting broader front faces than the previous experiment. The 8% local margins make the letters substantially fuller within the corridor, especially along the tall left portion of the arc, while maintaining the single-mesh proportional fitting method. The final 5-degree Z rotation gives the assembly a gentler rise from left to right. The fitted lettering remains approximately 13,284 triangles, below the provisional 25,000-triangle logo limit.

Days One continues to suit this stage of the design; no font change is recommended from this experiment.

## Comparison with 004-010

Compared with 004-010, the pitch decreases from 30 to 20 degrees and the final Z rotation decreases from 10 to 5 degrees. The new lettering occupies 84% rather than 70% of the local guide gap. As a result, 004-011 looks less steeply tilted, has larger and more readable letter faces, and uses the negative space between the guides more assertively. The composition remains inside the frame at the same camera distance, although the larger lettering makes the left end feel visually heavier and brings the upper portions of several letters close to the upper guide by design.
