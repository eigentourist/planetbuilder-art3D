# Nebula 001-009

## Interpretation

Retain experiment 001-008, including cloud01's +4-degree color-coordinate rotation. Apply a subtle low-frequency nonlinear distortion only to cloud03's color-field coordinates to bend geometric color contours. Leave all alpha masks, contrast, five-tap blur, placement, exposure, and cloud02 sampling unchanged.

## Observations

The cloud03 color warp operates cleanly. At a UV amplitude of 0.018 it gently bends broad transitions in the upper green region without making the texture visibly turbulent, changing the alpha silhouette, or disturbing the successful composite composition.

The previously observed angular green contour in cloud03 changes only slightly, and the marked parallelogram-like contour in the composite remains essentially intact. The conservative amplitude is therefore safe but insufficient as a complete correction. The persistence also leaves open the possibility that cloud02 or an interaction between multiple translucent layers contributes more strongly to the final artifact.

## Comparison with 001-008

Experiment 001-009 is almost identical to experiment 001-008 at normal preview scale. Close inspection shows mild nonlinear displacement within cloud03's colors, but no decisive reduction of the target composite contour. A later experiment could test a moderately higher cloud03 warp amplitude or isolate cloud02's color contribution before applying broader changes.
