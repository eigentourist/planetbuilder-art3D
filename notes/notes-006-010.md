# Notes for logo 006-010

## Interpretation

- Preserve the radius-ten baseline, 0.707-radian arc span, established spacing, positive 20-degree X and Y rotations, material, lighting, camera, and framing from 006-009.
- Apply the shared X/Y transform while the character meshes remain in their original horizontal line.
- After that transform is baked, replace each character's X/Y center position with its circular baseline position and rotate it around global Z to the local tangent.
- Preserve the character-center Z offsets created by the shared yaw so the left-near/right-far depth progression remains present.

## Observations

The outline follows the same readable radius-ten arc, but the noncommuting transform order changes the individual glyph facing. Tangent rotation now turns geometry that has already been pitched and yawed, producing a different distribution of visible side faces and bevel highlights from left to right. The red P and early PLANET forms show a somewhat stronger directional character, while the cream end remains broad and readable.

The logo remains centered, unclipped, and free of character overlap. The smoother material and large-area lighting continue to produce soft highlights without hotspots. Geometry remains approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains suitable; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 006-009

All numeric settings are identical; only transform order changes. Experiment 006-009 places and tangent-rotates each character on the arc before applying the shared X/Y matrix. Experiment 006-010 applies X/Y first and the arc/tangent transform afterward. The two retain nearly the same two-dimensional baseline silhouette, but 006-010 changes the effective local orientation and lighting response of each mesh while preserving the yaw-generated depth offsets.
