# Moon 001-001 Notes

## Interpretation

Create the first independent desert-moon asset as a radius-three UV sphere at the world origin, using the authored 4048×2024 equirectangular texture set. Use the base-color texture in sRGB; use ORM green and blue for roughness and metallic while retaining but not applying red/AO; combine the OpenGL tangent normal map with the height map through a Bump node using Normal strength 1.0, Bump strength 0.15, and Bump distance 0.1. Render in Eevee at 1200×675 with a centered 50 mm perspective camera, black background, and a moon diameter of approximately one quarter of the frame height. Use the specified 100/60/40-watt, 1.5-meter key/fill/rim rig.

The sphere uses 96 longitudinal segments and 64 rings, producing approximately 12,096 triangles. This is comfortably below the 25,000-triangle ceiling while providing a smooth silhouette at the intended display size.

## Observations

The generated moon is centered and its geometric framing is approximately the requested one-quarter frame height. However, the rendered preview is effectively black. At the selected light distances and scene scale, the specified 100-watt key, 60-watt fill, and 40-watt rim do not expose the surface sufficiently against the zero-strength black world.

Consequently, this preview does not yet fulfill the current design goal: the texture orientation, seam placement, material response, normal/bump detail, and silhouette quality cannot be evaluated visually. A subsequent experiment should preserve the specified light-power ratios while either raising all three powers substantially, increasing scene exposure, or moving the lights closer. Increasing exposure is likely the cleanest first test because it preserves the exact light powers and relative placement established here.

## Comparison with previous preview

There is no previous moon preview. This is the first experiment in the moon asset series and establishes its independent script, material, geometry, camera, lighting structure, and artifact naming.
