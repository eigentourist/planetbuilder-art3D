## Observations

The camera-right key produces stronger top-edge and face illumination across the middle and right-hand portion of the word. The near left-side depth faces, particularly around P and L, become more subdued, while the front faces remain readable through the fill light. The result has a clearer left-to-right lighting progression and continues to avoid harsh cast shadows and strong specular hotspots.

The standalone silhouette remains unclipped and unchanged. The scene contains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-006

Geometry, framing, fill, and rim lighting are identical. In 005-006 the camera-left key favored the nearer left-hand characters and emphasized their upper and side edges. In 005-007 the mirrored camera-right key distributes its strongest highlights toward BUILDER and gives the far end of the word more definition. The left side now carries greater shadow contrast, while the right side feels more evenly illuminated.
