# Nebula 001-007

## Interpretation

Conservatively increase contrast in all three historical grayscale masks so negative space approaches full transparency and therefore full black in the composite. Apply a small spatial blur after contrast adjustment to prevent the cleaner separation from producing unnaturally hard edges. Blur alpha structure only and preserve all RGB sampling, placement, exposure, and rotation settings. Retain B-spline interpolation for cloud03.

## Observations

The treatment works as intended. Dark gaps are cleaner and closer to full black, particularly through the central and upper-right negative space. Cloud masses read with slightly greater definition than in experiment 001-006. The five-tap blur keeps their transitions diffuse enough that the increased contrast does not create obvious cutout edges or dark RGB halos.

The composite becomes slightly darker and more restrained overall. This should help it remain subordinate to the logo, moon, and planet, although its final visibility should eventually be judged in the assembled title screen.

Cloud03's red/orange bubble-like color contour remains visible. Because this experiment modifies only mask contrast and alpha smoothing, its persistence reinforces the conclusion that the contour comes from the blurred color source. Correcting that particular artifact will require a localized change to cloud03's color-field coordinates or color modulation.

## Comparison with 001-006

Experiment 001-006 softened cloud03 globally but did little to improve negative-space separation. Experiment 001-007 produces darker, cleaner gaps across all three layers while retaining suitably soft cloud boundaries. It is a useful improvement to the mask treatment, but it does not solve the independent cloud03 color-contour issue.
