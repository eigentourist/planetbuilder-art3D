## Observations

The smaller yaw reduces left-to-right foreshortening while retaining a visible depth progression. BUILDER presents broader front faces and appears closer in scale to PLANET, making the word feel more typographically unified. The left side still advances toward the camera, but successive letters no longer recede as aggressively.

The larger 3%-allowance letterforms remain legible and unclipped. The lighting produces broad, soft face illumination and restrained edge highlights. The scene retains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-011

Only the shared lettering and guide yaw changes, decreasing from positive 15 to positive 10 degrees. Experiment 005-011 emphasizes depth and makes the right end visibly smaller and more receded. Experiment 005-012 moderates that perspective effect, broadens the visible faces on the right, and gives PLANETBUILDER a more even visual weight while preserving the rising silhouette and three-dimensional construction.
