# Notes for logo 006-009

## Interpretation

- Preserve the preferred radius-ten arc, tangent-aligned character placement, shared positive 20-degree X rotation, camera, material colors, and framing.
- Increase the shared Y rotation from positive 10 degrees to positive 20 degrees after arc placement.
- Use a camera-right key and left-rear large rim, restoring the lighting orientation from 006-007.
- Reduce roughness to 0.3 for red, 0.25 for orange, 0.2 for yellow, and 0.1 for cream.

## Observations

The stronger yaw makes the left-near/right-far depth progression more apparent and modestly foreshortens the cream end. The arc remains legible, centered, and unclipped. Lower roughness on the red and orange regions produces clearer bevel highlights and makes their response more consistent with the already-smooth yellow and cream regions.

The large light sources keep reflections broad despite the smoother material. No concentrated hotspots or harsh shadows are visible. Geometry remains approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains suitable; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 006-008

Experiment 006-008 uses positive 10-degree Y rotation, a camera-left key, a right-rear rim, and roughness values of 0.4/0.3/0.2/0.1. Experiment 006-009 doubles the yaw, reverses the key and rim positions, and lowers the red and orange roughness. The result has stronger spatial recession, brighter bevel response on the warm-color half, and lighting closer to 006-007's direction while retaining the radius-ten geometry.
