# Notes for logo 006-001

## Interpretation

- Begin design version 006 while preserving the final geometry, camera, lighting, and separate-character construction from 005-012.
- Create one shared object-space material coordinate system spanning the fitted logo from X=-4.62 to X=4.62 so the gradient remains continuous across all 13 meshes.
- Interpolate evenly through deep red, orange, yellow, and cream stops at 0%, 33.3%, 66.7%, and 100%.
- Interpolate roughness through 0.45, 0.35, 0.25, and 0.15 at the corresponding stops.
- Keep metallic at zero, emission disabled, and specular response restrained while retaining the existing large soft lights for bevel definition.

## Observations

The material forms a coherent logo-wide transition rather than restarting on each character. PLANET begins in a dark, saturated red and moves naturally into orange; the center passes through yellow, and the final BUILDER characters reach a warm cream. The changing roughness is visible as progressively clearer light response toward the right, particularly on the upper bevels, without creating strong point-like hotspots.

The darker red left end has greater shadow contrast than the previous uniform orange material, while the cream right end is visually prominent against the black background. The logo remains legible and unclipped, with 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-012

Geometry, framing, lighting, and camera placement are unchanged. Experiment 005-012 used one uniform orange material with a fixed roughness. Version 006-001 introduces both hue and roughness progression across the full word, creating stronger visual movement from the dark red foreground end toward the bright cream far end. Bevels remain softly illuminated, but their response is more apparent in the smoother yellow and cream regions.
