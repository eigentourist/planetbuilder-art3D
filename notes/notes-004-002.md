# Notes — 004-002

## Interpretation

Repeat the initial `PLANETBUILDER` fit using Days One in place of Goldman Bold. Preserve every other variable from experiment 004-001—including the guide geometry, equal center intervals, local height scaling, glyph rotation, extrusion, material, camera, and lighting—so the new preview functions as a direct font comparison.

## Observations

Days One fits the full title successfully and remains legible throughout the strong reduction in letter height toward the right. Its broad forms and substantial stroke weight hold up well at the small terminal scale. Rounded corners, softened counters, and the distinctive open construction of characters such as `A` give the word a friendlier and more explicitly mid-century science-fiction quality than the preceding font.

For the current artistic objective, Days One is the stronger of the two tested candidates. Goldman Bold has a sharper technological character, while Days One feels closer to the optimistic retro-futurism associated with *Forbidden Planet*. It also maintains a strong silhouette without becoming as severe or contemporary-looking.

The spacing weakness from experiment 004-001 remains because this controlled test intentionally retains equal glyph-center intervals. The narrow `I` still creates excessive negative space, and the rhythm across `BUILDER` remains uneven. This preview should be used to choose between font personalities before implementing glyph-aware spacing.

No GLB or texture files were produced. The characters remain editable Blender text objects.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-004-001.png`, `logo-004-002.png` changes only the typeface. Days One produces rounder terminals and counters, a more playful `A`, and a softer overall rhythm. Both fonts fit the envelope, but Days One currently aligns more closely with the stated 1950s retro science-fiction influence.
