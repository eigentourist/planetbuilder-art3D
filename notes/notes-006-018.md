# Notes for logo 006-018

## Interpretation

Keep the established Days One character meshes, spacing, material gradient, roughness values, shared X and Y rotations, lighting, and camera unchanged. Increase only the circular baseline radius from 15 to 20 Blender units, placing and tangent-aligning the characters on that arc before applying the shared rotations.

## Observations

The 20-unit radius produces a gentler baseline while preserving a clearly perceptible rise from left to right. The word spans approximately 0.354 radians, or 20.3 degrees, compared with approximately 27 degrees at radius 15. The reduced curvature makes the word feel more unified and less strongly bowed, while the individual character tangent rotations remain coherent.

The warm color and increasing-roughness gradients remain intact, and the soft three-light rig continues to reveal the bevels without obvious hot spots. The logo remains well below the provisional 25,000-triangle limit at approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with logo 006-017

The flatter arc is the sole intended design change. Relative to 006-017, the left side rises less sharply toward the middle and the character orientations vary less across the word. The right end consequently appears more level, while the overall framing and horizontal footprint remain close to the previous preview. Material response, color progression, foreshortening, and lighting are otherwise unchanged.
