# Notes for logo 006-017

## Interpretation

Keep the geometry, arc placement, shared rotations, color gradient, camera, and three-light rig from experiment 006-016 unchanged. Lower the four evenly distributed roughness stops while retaining their left-to-right increase: red 0.05, orange 0.10, yellow 0.15, and cream 0.20.

## Observations

The preview retains the established rising arc, foreshortened presentation, warm color progression, and soft three-light modeling. The lower roughness values give the bevel highlights slightly sharper definition, particularly across the red and orange characters, while the large area lights continue to prevent harsh hotspots. The cream end remains comparatively soft because it still has the highest roughness.

The logo remains well below the provisional 25,000-triangle limit at approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One continues to suit the broad, retro-futurist character of the design; no font change is recommended for this experiment.

## Comparison with logo 006-016

The only intended change is a reduction of 0.05 at every roughness stop, from 0.10–0.25 in 006-016 to 0.05–0.20 in 006-017. Composition, silhouette, color placement, and lighting remain visually unchanged. The difference is subtle but visible as slightly clearer and brighter reflections along some upper and left-facing bevels, most notably toward the red/orange side. The result still satisfies the instruction to keep highlights soft and avoid hot spots.
