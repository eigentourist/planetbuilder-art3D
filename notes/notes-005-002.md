# Notes for logo 005-002

## Interpretation

- Preserve the shape, character spacing, three-light rig, and shared rotations from 005-001.
- Continue moving all character centers positive 15 degrees around Y using the shared lettering pivot, retaining the left-near/right-far depth progression.
- After that shared transformation, apply a negative 15-degree world-Y rotation to each character's geometry around its own bounding-box center.
- Keep the guides at the shared positive 15-degree Y orientation so they follow the positional plane of the lettering.

## Observations

The 13 character faces and side edges now read as mutually parallel while their centers retain the intended depth progression. The foreground-overlap impression between P and L and between L and A is reduced. Front faces remain broad and legible across more of the word, and the lighting produces a more consistent upper-edge highlight. No obvious inter-character collisions or guide clipping are visible. The mesh total remains approximately 13,284 triangles.

The right-hand characters sit visually close to the upper guide, particularly near the final letters, so that region may merit attention during later shape or spacing refinement. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-001

In 005-001, shared yaw changed both character positions and their facing direction, making successive letters appear to stand behind their left-hand neighbors. Experiment 005-002 retains the same positional depth ordering but cancels the shared facing-direction change at each character center. The result looks flatter at the individual-letter level while the complete logo still occupies a visibly three-dimensional arrangement. The three-light scheme and framing remain unchanged, making the yaw difference directly observable.
