Preserve all centerline geometry, positions, the upper line's 5-degree clockwise rotation, color, framing, and black background from experiment 002-004. Reduce the diameter of both contour strokes by approximately 50 percent to make the negative space between them easier to evaluate precisely.

## Observations

The preview fulfills the instruction. Both line diameters are exactly half their previous size, while the underlying paths remain unchanged. The lighter visual weight shifts attention away from the guides themselves and toward the enclosed negative-space silhouette.

The thinner contours make several geometric qualities clearer: the left opening is broad, the space tapers continuously toward the right, the upper boundary rises to a gentle crest and then descends, and the lower boundary settles into a horizontal finish. The contours remain separate with a compact right-side gap. The upper and lower endpoints still differ in angle and horizontal extent, but those differences are now easier to assess accurately. No font recommendation is relevant at this contour-development stage.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-002-004.png`, the centerlines and their spacing are identical; only the stroke thickness has changed. Reducing the thickness by 50 percent increases the visible edge-to-edge negative space and removes the heavier band-like appearance. Experiment 002-005 is consequently a clearer technical study of the prospective logo envelope and a better reference for precise contour refinement.
