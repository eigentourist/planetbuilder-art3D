# Planet 001-001 Notes

## Interpretation

Create the first independent forest-planet asset as a radius-three UV sphere using the authored 4110×2055 equirectangular texture set. Apply the base-color image in sRGB; use ORM green and blue for roughness and metallic while retaining but not applying red/AO; combine the OpenGL tangent normal map with the height map through a Bump node using Normal strength 1.0, Bump strength 0.15, and Bump distance 0.1.

Render in Eevee at 1200×675 with a centered 50 mm perspective camera, black background, and a target planet diameter of roughly one third of frame height. Use the specified 1000/600/400-watt key/fill/rim rig with 1.5-meter area lights. A 96-segment, 64-ring sphere yields approximately 12,096 triangles, comfortably below the 25,000-triangle ceiling.

## Observations

The generated planet is centered, clearly spherical, and visually readable against the black background. Its dark forest-green color, brighter organic-looking clusters, and broad surface patterns suggest a lush or biologically active world. The combined normal and bump maps produce substantial small-scale relief, particularly across the lower hemisphere. No obvious longitudinal texture seam appears on the visible side, and the 12,096-triangle sphere has a smooth silhouette at this scale.

The planet appears slightly smaller than a full one third of frame height, although it is reasonably close to that approximate target. Illumination is sufficient to reveal the material, but the result is quite dark overall, especially along the left and lower edges. Some bright details stand out strongly from the surrounding green surface; these appear to originate substantially in the authored imagery rather than as broad specular hot spots from the soft area lights.

This preview fulfills the goal of establishing the forest planet as a separate, textured 3D asset and provides a useful baseline. Later experiments could bring the camera modestly closer, increase overall exposure, alter the texture-facing orientation, or reduce normal/bump strength after evaluating the intended title-screen composition.

## Comparison with previous preview

There is no previous forest-planet preview. This is the first experiment in the independent planet asset series and establishes its script, geometry, PBR-oriented material wiring, camera, lighting rig, and artifact naming. The moon and logo previews are separate asset series and are not direct predecessors for planet experiment numbering.
