# Notes for logo 005-008

## Interpretation

- Preserve the standalone lettering, closer camera, camera-right key, fill light, materials, geometry, and framing from 005-007.
- Keep the rim light behind the logo at Z=-5.0 and above it at Y=5.5.
- Move the rim toward the left side to X=-5.5, retaining its 0.5 relative intensity, medium 4.5-unit size, warm color, and aim at the logo center.

## Observations

The left-biased rear rim introduces subtle additional separation along the nearer left-side contours, particularly around P, L, and A. The camera-right key continues to carry the strongest front and upper-edge illumination across the middle and right of the word. Together they produce a more balanced opposing-light arrangement without harsh shadows or prominent specular hotspots.

The rim effect remains restrained rather than outlining the entire silhouette, which is consistent with the soft-light objective. The logo remains unclipped and contains 13 character meshes totaling approximately 13,284 triangles. Days One remains appropriate; no font change is recommended.

## Comparison with 005-007

Only the rim light's horizontal position changes. In 005-007 it sat just right of center at X=1.0; in 005-008 it moves to X=-5.5 while retaining its height and depth. The new position gives the left characters slightly clearer rear-edge definition and complements the camera-right key more effectively. The overall difference is subtle because the rim remains behind the shallow extrusion and uses a large, soft source.
