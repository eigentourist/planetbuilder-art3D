# Notes — 003-001

## Interpretation

Begin design version 003 by converting the approved version 002 centerlines into temporary 3D ribbon guides. Preserve the established XY geometry and 5-degree upper-line rotation. Use hidden 3D Bézier curves as editable centerlines and generate flat ribbon meshes with a width and Z depth of 0.105 Blender units, rounded ends, 2 mm edge bevels, and three bevel segments. Replace the emission material with a rough, low-specular Principled BSDF cream material. Present the result through a centered 60 mm perspective camera with a soft-white area light above the camera and a black environment-only background. Do not export a GLB.

## Observations

The geometry portion of the refactor is successful. Both paths produce closed solid meshes with flat front and back faces, side walls, rounded terminal silhouettes, and subtle beveled edges. The generated ribbons retain the spacing, taper, and trajectory established in version 002. Their modifiers were applied, normals were regenerated, UV maps were created, and each visible ribbon has its own geometric-center origin. The source Bézier centerlines remain in the `.blend` as non-rendering 3D guide objects.

The straight-on perspective view preserves the established framing and shows the rounded ends clearly, but it naturally reveals little of the 105 mm depth. The new physically lit material also appears substantially darker and browner than the prior emission-based cream. Although its base color is unchanged, the single area light and zero-strength black environment do not provide enough frontal illumination to retain the orange-cream-soda appearance. A later experiment should increase or reposition the area light, adjust exposure, or add a restrained non-exported fill light while preserving the material's low-specular character.

No GLB or texture files were created because the current objects are temporary guides. The ribbon meshes remain far below the provisional 25,000-triangle logo budget. No font recommendation is relevant during this guide-conversion phase.

## Comparison

Compared with `logo-002-005.png`, `logo-003-001.png` preserves the same centerline shapes, relative spacing, thin apparent width, and black framing while replacing planar emission strokes with physically modeled solids. Rounded caps replace the earlier flat ends. The new perspective camera produces little visible distortion from its centered, long-lens position, which is appropriate for maintaining the established silhouette. The most significant visual change is the darker material response: version 002 displayed a bright warm cream consistently, while version 003 now responds to scene lighting and therefore needs further illumination refinement.
